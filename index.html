Live data comes from Sound Transit's official real-time feed (OneBusAway Puget Sound). The app starts with the shared TEST key, which works but is shared by many people and can get rate-limited.

For the most reliable results, get your own free key: email oba_api_key@soundtransit.org with your first and last name, your email address, and a note that you've read and agree to Sound Transit's Transit Data Terms of Use. Paste the key here when it arrives.
